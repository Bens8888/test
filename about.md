# AutoLayout

Pick a song, press **Generate**, and AutoLayout designs a complete layout for it: structure,
pacing, gamemodes, speed changes, difficulty curve, blocks, spikes, orbs and triggers — built
around the music and verified by a built-in physics bot so every generated level is beatable.

Open it from the **music note button** in the editor's top-right menu (or `Ctrl+Alt+L` /
`Cmd+Alt+L`).

- Fully automatic mode: the click rhythm comes from the song itself.
- Click-recording mode: play along, and the layout is built around your clicks.
- Every layout is playtested by the bot; anything it can't beat is repaired or regenerated.
- Everything is applied as one undo step on its own editor layer, and the level is backed up first.
